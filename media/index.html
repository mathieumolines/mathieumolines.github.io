Media & public writing

Alongside academic papers, I write for a general audience about what research tells us on management, motivation and ethics in public service, particularly in the police. These pieces are in French.

Le Monde

  • (2023) Le sentiment d’être perçu négativement et de se trouver souvent en danger engendre une distance entre les policiers et la société, Op-ed (tribune), Le Monde, 20 July 2023.
    Op-ed on the distance between police officers and society, fuelled by the feeling of being perceived negatively and often being in danger.

The Conversation France

  • (2025) La police française face à la honte : entre crise identitaire et renouveau éthique, with Anthony Perrier.
    How media scandals affect police officers’ professional identity, and how shame can become a lever for ethical renewal.
  • (2021) Qu’est-ce qui motive les policiers ?, with Matthieu Mifsud.
    What a year-long study of 1,400 French police officers reveals about public service motivation and how to sustain it.
  • (2019) C’est « mon » programme de soin, ou comment l’appropriation améliore la prise en charge du diabète, with Matthieu Mifsud.
    How a sense of psychological ownership improves treatment adherence among people with diabetes (study of 502 patients).
  • (2018) Police, une remise en question nécessaire de l’organisation et du management, with Pierre-Yves Sanséau.
    Why the bureaucratic organization and management style of the French police need rethinking, and concrete proposals.
  • (2018) Stress et suicide dans la police, l’organisation policière en question, with Pierre-Yves Sanséau.
    How the organization and management of the police contribute to stress, based on a study of 718 officers in 70 teams.
  • (2017) Comment la blockchain va changer la gouvernance des entreprises.
    How blockchain technology could reshape corporate governance.
